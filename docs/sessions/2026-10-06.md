# Session log · 2026-10-06 (TOKEN2049 Origins, day 1)

Decisions, feedback and open questions that matter for the code. Non-code notes (pitch, people, logistics) live in the team's private notes.

## Decisions
- **Name:** clim (the market's weather).
- **Product:** storm insurance for Uniswap v4 LPs. A Chainlink CRE "risk desk" publishes multi-venue ETH realized volatility on-chain every 30 s; a v4 hook sets a symmetric dynamic LP fee per swap: `fee = clamp(max(floor, eta * sigma * sqrt(blockTime/2)), floor, cap)`, where `eta = 1/P* - 0.824` (Milionis-Moallemi-Roughgarden 2023, Nezlobin-Tassy 2025). The floor is the pair's market fee tier (5 bp for ETH/USDC).
- **P\* (target share of arbitraged blocks): 20% or 30%.** It is decided in the lab against a competing 5 bp pool before the hook is deployed, because hook parameters are immutable.
- **Chain:** Ethereum Sepolia for the hackathon. Uniswap v4 and CRE are both available there, and 12 s blocks make the formula produce readable fees.
  - Not Solana: there are no v4 hooks, we would need our own AMM program, and Meteora already ships volatility-based fees.
  - Later, CRE can write the same sigma to other chains, including Solana ("one desk, many chains").
- **Team:** solo builder for now, with delegation later. The plan marks tasks that can be handed off.
- **Frontend:** a basic, functional dashboard built alongside the rest, to be restyled later.
- **The plan is not frozen.** Change it as soon as the build or new research requires it, and log the change here.

## Feedback received
### Chainlink mentor (idea pitch)
- **Reaction:** likes the idea.
- **Q1: "How is the fee changed and computed?"** He did not see the mechanism.
  - Answer: the fee is never changed by a transaction. On every swap, Uniswap calls the hook's `beforeSwap`. The hook reads the latest sigma from `RiskDesk`, computes the fee and returns it with `OVERRIDE_FEE_FLAG`.
  - Action: show this as one picture in the deck and the README (a sigma curve with the fee stepping under it).
- **Q2: "How do you implement or change fees on a pool that is already live, and is it possible?"** Answer to be verified and written in `docs/faq.md`:
  - a v4 pool's fee mode and hook are fixed in its `PoolKey` at initialization, so an existing static-fee pool cannot become dynamic or gain a hook: you create a new pool and migrate liquidity;
  - a dynamic-fee pool's hook can override the fee per swap (`beforeSwap`) or store a new fee (`PoolManager.updateDynamicLPFee`, callable by the hook only);
  - for an existing dynamic-fee DEX (e.g. Fables), the lightest integration is for their hook or keeper to read `RiskDesk.state()`;
  - clim's own parameters are immutable, so changing P\* means a new hook and pool.
- **Q3: "What do you think of CRE, is it easy to implement?"** We answered from the design phase. We keep a hands-on friction log during the build: `docs/feedback/cre-friction-log.md`.

## Product and frontend framing (decided)
- clim is **infrastructure**, not a swap app. It has two pieces: the CRE risk desk (`RiskDesk`, a public on-chain "market weather" feed) and the hook (`ClimHook`). Traders keep swapping on Uniswap or aggregators, and the fee applies automatically.
- It does not plug into existing pools: a live v4 pool cannot gain a hook. There are two ways in:
  - launch new pools with the clim hook;
  - existing dynamic-fee DEXs (e.g. Fables) read `RiskDesk` from their own hook or keeper.
- The frontend is the **public terminal of the risk desk**:
  - live weather (sigma, sources, age, mode);
  - current fee of the clim pool vs a static pool, and the fee-follows-weather chart;
  - proof: P_trade predicted vs observed, and LP P&L clim vs static;
  - a showcase for judges: `/lab` (Feb 4 replay, backtest) and `/how` (explainer + FAQ).
- It is not a swap UI. Optional later: an "LP" view (connect wallet, add liquidity to the clim pool on Sepolia, see P&L vs static).
- The frontend is being built in a separate repo (DVB-ANS/clim-front, private) that will become `clim/app/` as-is. (Confirmed by the fixer pass: master plan Task 14 imports it with `git subtree`.)
