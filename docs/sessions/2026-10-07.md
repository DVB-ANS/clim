# Session log · 2026-10-07 (clim-front)

Same conventions as `2026-10-06.md`: decisions, deviations from plan 05 and surprises, with the headings of `clim/docs/sessions/`, so the lines can be merged there.

## Decisions
- **Art direction: Ventriloc (maintainer's choice).** The look copies refero's Ventriloc style (styles.refero.design, style f99aca3e; site ventriloc.ca), adapted to clim: warm paper (Fog, Ash, Ivory), Graphite text, one Ember orange for highlights and Brass as its counterpoint, headings in a neo-grotesque at weight 400, pill navigation, 20 px data cards, Ventriloc's stacked service panels, multiplayer cursors on the data cards, Brass page transition, graphite footer with a giant glyph (here the degree sign of the `clim°` wordmark). Fonts: Inter Tight stands in for the commercial PolySans, over Inter; Doto is the dot-matrix face of the CL-1 screen. Two rounds came before it and were rejected by the maintainer (static canvas mock-ups, then live prototypes after teenage engineering, Modern Treasury and Felt).
- **Entity colours change (deviation from plan 05):** Ventriloc allows no chromatic colour beyond Ember and Brass, so V is Ember, S is Graphite (always dashed) and σ is Brass, instead of plan 05's V blue, S orange, σ aqua. They differ in lightness as well as hue. One place to change them: `src/app/globals.css` (`--clim-v`, `--clim-s`, `--clim-sigma`).
- **Routes (deviation from plan 05's route table):** `/` is now the landing page and the dashboard moves to `/app`; `/replay`, `/lab`, `/how`, `/swap` and `/lp` keep their URLs. Two route groups do it: `src/app/(site)` (landing) and `src/app/(app)` (the app's header and pages). Links to the dashboard in the deck or plan 06 should use `/app`.
- **Landing content:** every number comes from `useClimData("live")` and says "simulated" until the contracts are deployed. It brings in the maintainer's four picks in Ventriloc's language: the CL-1 instrument with its dot-matrix storm (a hero card), the live pipeline diagram (venues and DVOL → Chainlink CRE DON → RiskDesk → ClimHook → V and S, a packet on every report), the isobars (the low deepens with σ) with a ticker of the latest reports, and the dotted venue map. The desk's checks (venues agree, heartbeat, sources) and status bars come from the new `src/lib/desk.ts` (tested).
- **Launch transition (maintainer's choice, "storm zoom + live boot"):** a real link to `/app`; a plain click plays the Brass panel for about a second (the storm zooms into its eye while three lines name the block, the report and `quoteFee()`), then the app opens and the panel fades. No fake waiting; with reduced motion it is a short fade.
- **Venue map data:** generated once by `scripts/make-world-dots.mjs` (`dotted-map`, dev dependency) into `src/lib/worldDots.ts`, so the page ships dots, not the library. Venues sit at approximate regions (Coinbase US East, Kraken US West, Binance and Hyperliquid Tokyo, Deribit London), and the map says so.
- **Chainlink palette (maintainer's feedback on the Ventriloc version):** Ventriloc keeps the layout and the type, Chainlink's design system (chain.link) gives the colours: Chainlink Blue `#0847f7` for highlights and primary actions, its gray ramp and navy, orange 500 for σ. Entities become V Chainlink Blue, S gray (dashed), σ orange; this supersedes the Ember/Graphite/Brass line above.
- **The app is "the desk", dark:** the `(app)` layout carries `.theme-desk`, the CL-1 screen's navy and Chainlink's dark grays, so the app reads as a different place from the light landing (maintainer's request). RainbowKit uses its dark theme.
- **Landing as a showcase (maintainer's request):** no buttons in the hero or the closing panel. The ways in are the header's blue pill and the CL-1 card itself; the launch zooms into its screen (a copy of the LCD over the hero's, scaled around the storm's eye by a compositor transform, the desk's ground fading in), then `/app` opens underneath; this replaces the Brass panel above. Scroll-driven transitions (CSS `animation-timeline`, static where unsupported): the hero rises in, a stacked panel recedes as the next covers it, the closing panel grows to full width while its giant degree sign turns in.
- **Logos:** the partner strip shows the stack's marks, generated by `scripts/make-logos.mjs` from `@web3icons/core` (MIT, dev dependency) and recoloured to `currentColor`: Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, Chainlink, Ethereum, Uniswap. Hyperliquid and Deribit have no open mark there (`simple-icons` has neither, nor Uniswap or Kraken), so they stay wordmarks rather than redrawn logos.
- **Chart tooltips (maintainer's feedback):** one shared, discreet tooltip that lists only the curves and glides between points; the charts no longer share a `syncId`, so only the hovered chart shows one. The weather chart's swap dots had joined the payload near each dot (the box resized and flickered, and the raw `t` leaked as "bp"). It now caps its lines at 600 points and its dots at 300.
- **Swap and liquidity as DEX cards (maintainer's request):** pool cards with the fee now, amount boxes with token pills and a flip button, fee, price and route rows, a full-range band, and position cards with the P&L against the twin pool as two bars; the transaction logic is unchanged.

## Surprises
- **Stacked sticky panels must fit the window:** a sticky panel taller than the viewport hides its own bottom behind the next one. The stacking applies only from 820 px of window height (and 768 px of width); below that the panels simply follow each other.
- **Canvases do not read CSS variables:** `fillStyle` and `font` need resolved colours and family names, so the canvases read the tokens once through `getComputedStyle` (`src/components/site/canvasTokens.ts`) and keep every colour in `globals.css`.
- **Next 16 keeps smooth scrolling during navigation:** with `scroll-behavior: smooth` on `<html>`, every route change glided back to the top. `data-scroll-behavior="smooth"` on `<html>` restores the jump (in-page anchors still glide).
